Simulation Explanation

This simulation demonstrates how bandwidth limitations affect digital baseband transmission:

  • Input Signal: A sequence of binary digits (0s and 1s) represented as a Non-Return-to-Zero (NRZ) waveform.
  • Wide Bandwidth Channel: A low-pass channel with relatively high cutoff frequency, allowing most signal harmonics to pass through.
  • Narrow Bandwidth Channel: A low-pass channel with a lower cutoff frequency, which filters out more harmonics.
  • Received Signal: The output after a decision circuit that converts the analog signal back to digital based on a threshold.

Key observations:

  • Narrower bandwidth causes more intersymbol interference (ISI) due to signal spreading.
  • Signal transitions become less sharp with decreased bandwidth.
  • Bit errors are more likely to occur in bandwidth-limited channels.
