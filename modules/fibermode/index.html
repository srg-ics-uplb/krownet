Mode Information

Single mode fiber has a small core (8-10 μm) and transmits light in only one mode, resulting in less dispersion and higher bandwidth over long distances. It typically operates at wavelengths of 1310-1550 nm.

Pulse Dispersion

Dispersion Effects

Single mode fiber exhibits minimal pulse dispersion, resulting in high bandwidth and allowing for longer transmission distances.
