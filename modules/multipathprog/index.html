Multipath Propagation Effects

In wireless networks, signals can take multiple paths from transmitter to receiver due to:

  • Reflection: Signals bounce off surfaces like walls and furniture
  • Diffraction: Signals bend around obstacles
  • Scattering: Signals disperse when hitting rough surfaces

These effects can cause signal interference, leading to:

  • Delay spread: Different arrival times of signal components
  • Fading: Signal strength fluctuations
  • Inter-Symbol Interference (ISI): Overlapping symbols in the received signal

This simulation demonstrates how multipath propagation works in a simplified 2D environment.
