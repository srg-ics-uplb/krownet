About Harmonic Composite Signals

A harmonic composite signal consists of a fundamental frequency and its integer multiples (harmonics). This visualization shows:

  • Time Domain: The waveform as it varies over time (1 second duration shown)
  • Component Breakdown: Each harmonic sinusoid displayed separately
  • Frequency Domain: Discrete frequency peaks at each harmonic frequency
